01 — Professional Profile

Engineering across the entire technology stack.

I combine software engineering, infrastructure, networking and security to build technology systems that can be developed, deployed, operated and maintained in real production environments.

I am a Computer Engineer with a practical, end-to-end approach to technology engineering. My experience spans application development, backend systems, databases, Linux administration, networking, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, mobile development and systems integration.

Rather than treating software and infrastructure as separate disciplines, I work across the boundary between them. I understand that a production application depends not only on code, but also on databases, operating systems, DNS, networking, authentication, firewalls, certificates, infrastructure capacity and reliable deployment.

This perspective allows me to investigate problems at their actual source instead of limiting troubleshooting to a single technology layer.

My approach is practical: understand the requirement, design the solution, build it, secure it, deploy it, monitor it and improve it.
